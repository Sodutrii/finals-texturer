ft_exporter (Finals Texturer's Export Now helper) uses these libraries, shipped as the DLLs in this folder:

- CUE4Parse and CUE4Parse-Conversion (https://github.com/FabianFG/CUE4Parse), Apache License 2.0
- Newtonsoft.Json, MIT License
- SkiaSharp, MIT License; SixLabors.ImageSharp, Six Labors Split License
- AssetRipper.TextureDecoder, MIT License; SharpGLTF, MIT License
- and their dependencies (see each package's licence on nuget.org)

Not shipped: the Oodle, zlib and Detex libraries and the game's AES key / mappings are read from the
user's own FModel installation at runtime.
